Review worksheet 2: geometry and measurement units

Published 17 September 2026

Open PDF

The PDF is in Romanian.

  • For multiple-choice items, circle the letter of the only correct answer.
  • For items with parts a) and b), write complete solutions in your notebook.

A. Point, line, segment, angle

  1. Points $A$, $B$ and $C$ are collinear, in this order, $AB = 7\text{ cm}$ and $BC = 5\text{ cm}$. The length of segment $AC$ is:

    • a) $12\text{ cm}$
    • b) $2\text{ cm}$
    • c) $35\text{ cm}$
    • d) $7\text{ cm}$

  2. Point $M$ is the midpoint of segment $AB$ and $AM = 4,5\text{ cm}$. The length of segment $AB$ is:

    • a) $9\text{ cm}$
    • b) $2,25\text{ cm}$
    • c) $4,5\text{ cm}$
    • d) $13,5\text{ cm}$

  3. An angle measuring $135{^\circ}$ is:

    • a) acute
    • b) right
    • c) obtuse
    • d) straight

  4. The result of $35{^\circ}48' + 24{^\circ}27'$ is:

    • a) $60{^\circ}15'$
    • b) $59{^\circ}75'$
    • c) $59{^\circ}15'$
    • d) $60{^\circ}75'$

B. Measurement units. Perimeters, areas, volumes

  1. The length $3,2\text{ m}$, expressed in centimetres, is:

    • a) $32\text{ cm}$
    • b) $320\text{ cm}$
    • c) $3200\text{ cm}$
    • d) $0,32\text{ cm}$

  2. The area $2,5\text{ dm}^{2}$, expressed in square centimetres, is:

    • a) $25\text{ cm}^{2}$
    • b) $250\text{ cm}^{2}$
    • c) $2500\text{ cm}^{2}$
    • d) $0,25\text{ cm}^{2}$

  3. A square has side $7\text{ cm}$. The area of the square is:

    • a) $28\text{ cm}^{2}$
    • b) $49\text{ cm}^{2}$
    • c) $14\text{ cm}^{2}$
    • d) $21\text{ cm}^{2}$

  4. The greatest of the lengths $0,5\text{ km}$, $480\text{ m}$, $4900\text{ dm}$ and $51000\text{ cm}$ is:

    • a) $0,5\text{ km}$
    • b) $480\text{ m}$
    • c) $4900\text{ dm}$
    • d) $51000\text{ cm}$

  5. A cube-shaped container with edge $20\text{ cm}$ is full of water. The amount of water in the container is:

    • a) $8$ litres
    • b) $80$ litres
    • c) $0,8$ litres
    • d) $800$ litres

C. Problems

  1. Work in your notebook, using a graduated ruler and a protractor.

    a) Construct a segment $AB$ of length $6\text{ cm}$ and mark its midpoint, $M$. Write the length of segment $MB$.

    b) Construct angle $MAC$ measuring $50{^\circ}$ and state the type of this angle.

  2. A rectangle is $1,2\text{ dm}$ long, and its width is $4\text{ cm}$ less than its length.

    a) Calculate the perimeter of the rectangle, in centimetres.

    b) Calculate the area of the rectangle, in square centimetres and in square decimetres.

  3. The ray $OC$ lies inside the right angle $AOB$, and $m(\sphericalangle AOC) = 37{^\circ}45'$.

    a) Calculate the measure of angle $COB$.

    b) State whether angles $AOC$ and $COB$ are congruent. Justify your answer.

  4. The floor of a room is a rectangle $5\text{ m}$ long and $4,2\text{ m}$ wide.

    a) Calculate the area of the floor.

    b) One box of parquet flooring covers $2,5\text{ m}^{2}$. Find the minimum number of boxes needed to cover the whole floor.

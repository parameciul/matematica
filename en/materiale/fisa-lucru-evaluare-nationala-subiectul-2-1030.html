Summary

Subject II of the National Evaluation tests plane and solid geometry with six items that come with figures: segments, angles, triangles and quadrilaterals, the circle, areas and solids. The worksheet has five items of each type, each with its own drawing. You pick the letter of the correct answer and check it on the page.

Frequently asked questions

What does Subject II of the National Evaluation ask for?

Six multiple-choice geometry items worth 5 points each, usually with a figure next to them: collinear points and segments, angles, triangles and quadrilaterals, the circle, areas and a solid. You use the data in the statement and in the figure together.

How do I use the figure in a geometry item?

Mark every piece of data from the statement on the figure: lengths, angles, midpoints, right angles. Then look for a configuration you recognise, such as a right triangle, a midline or an inscribed angle. Do not measure on the drawing: the figure is not to scale, so the result is calculated, not read off.
